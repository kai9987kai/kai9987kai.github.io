Research Notes

What the model does, and what the research informs

Planning: Beam search retains promising action sequences, counts revisits within each imagined path, models single-use energy pickups, and applies a terminal Q-value. The original random-shooting planner remains available. Both execute only the first action; the drawn path is the actual scored rollout.

Model limits: Agents learn a reward map with exponential averaging and tabular Q updates. Geometry, hazards, and energy locations are known. Future hazard motion is not learned. Novelty uses actual visit counts; uncertainty is an observation-confidence heuristic, not a calibrated probability. Sharing is distance weighted, not neural attention.

Research: TD-MPC2 informs receding-horizon planning; Dreamer 4 motivates separating imagination from real-world evaluation. This browser lab does not reproduce their architectures. rliable motivates multiple seeds and interval estimates.

Reading results: Coverage is the mean fraction of cells sensed per agent. Model MAE includes unseen cells. The chart and metrics CSV retain the last 360 steps; cumulative agent totals survive in snapshots. A paired bootstrap interval describes uncertainty across the selected seeds, not universal superiority.
