Research-informed design notes

This release is deliberately a transparent educational abstraction. It combines sequence, chromatin accessibility, DNA methylation, histone acetylation, transcription-factor activity, gene products, oxidative damage, repair, energy constraints and an interpretable tabular reinforcement-learning controller. Parameters are dimensionless and chosen for stable, explorable behaviour—not fitted to a real cell.

What changed

  • Chromatin accessibility is continuous and jointly influenced by methylation, acetylation, local damage, pioneer-factor activity and neighbouring loci.
  • Gene expression is coupled to accessible promoters and enhancers, with mRNA and protein synthesis/decay separated in time.
  • Damage and repair compete for ATP; radiation and oxidative environments create different lesion profiles.
  • Cell age, epigenetic drift, lineage inheritance and a reproducible seed support repeatable experiments.
  • The agent now learns from state transitions and change in fitness, uses action masks, decaying exploration, and reports its Q values.

Research directions reflected in the simulator

  • Systematic epigenome editing and context-dependent transcriptional effects (Nature Genetics, 2024)
  • EpiTrace: clock-like chromatin accessibility for cell age and lineage tracing (Nature Biotechnology, 2025)
  • ChromatinHD: multiscale links between accessibility and expression (Nature Communications, 2025)
  • GET: a foundation model connecting sequence, motifs, accessibility and transcription (Nature, 2025)
  • RegVelo: gene-regulatory-informed cellular dynamics (Cell, 2026)

These papers motivate the simulator’s structure, but they do not validate its numeric outputs. A future scientific version would need parameter estimation from a defined organism, cell type, assay and experimental dataset.
