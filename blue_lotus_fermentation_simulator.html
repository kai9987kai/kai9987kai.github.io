Research-oriented browser simulation of Saccharomyces beer fermentation, plant extraction, and rule-based candidate biotransformations for Nymphaea caerulea. The plant-chemistry layer is intentionally uncertainty-aware: predicted compounds are hypotheses, not analytical identifications.

Model assumptions

Primary kinetics: Monod-style substrate limitation with empirical temperature, oxygen, ethanol-inhibition and carrying-capacity terms. Secondary metabolites are proxy trajectories, not universal mechanistic equations.

Plant chemistry: the app separates extraction from transformation. Rule-generated products are conservative candidates generated only from defined transformation templates. An unresolved formula is shown rather than invented.

Uncertainty: Monte Carlo mode samples yeast kinetics, yields, plant composition, extraction rates and transformation rates. Percentile bands reflect model uncertainty only; they do not include unknown contamination, cultivar, storage or analytical error.

Bibliography / provenance

Standalone educational/research simulator. No external libraries, APIs, analytics, or runtime network requests.
