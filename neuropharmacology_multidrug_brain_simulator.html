Multisystem Neuropharmacology Sandbox

Research-informed mechanistic simulation of simultaneous LSD, Semax, nicotine, blue-lotus alkaloids, ketamine, and a generic neural cell graft. Outputs are normalized systems-level states, not predictions of a safe or effective human exposure.

single-file • offline • uncertainty-aware
Safety boundary: direct injection of psychoactive compounds or unspecialized stem cells into brain tissue can cause catastrophic hemorrhage, infection, seizures, tissue destruction, immune injury, abnormal growth, coma or death. This model intentionally contains no injection coordinates, dose conversion, concentrations, volumes, needle parameters, or procedural instructions. “Exposure” is a dimensionless target-engagement scenario variable.

Model equations

The simulator is a bounded nonlinear ODE system. All states are dimensionless and normalized around a baseline of 1 where appropriate.

R_HT2A = Hill(LSD) × [1 − nuciferine antagonism]
R_nAChR = Hill(nicotine) × [1 − desensitization]
R_NMDAblock = Hill(ketamine)
d(desens)/dt = k_on·nicotine·(1−desens) − k_off·desens
d(glutamate)/dt = cortical 5-HT2A drive + NMDAR-disinhibition + α7 drive − recovery
d(GABA)/dt = nicotine transient drive − ketamine disinhibition term − recovery
d(BDNF)/dt = LSD-pathway + ketamine/AMPAR + Semax + graft trophic contribution − turnover
d(mTOR)/dt = AMPAR/BDNF coupling − turnover
d(inflammation)/dt = injury + immune mismatch + network stress − resolution
d(graft viability)/dt = −inflammation − network stress + bounded trophic survival term

Interactions are modeled at pathway level rather than by adding subjective drug effects. Monte-Carlo coefficient perturbation represents uncertainty, especially for Semax, blue lotus, and graft biology.

Scientific references used for mechanism selection
  1. Vargas et al., Science (2023): intracellular 5-HT₂A receptors and psychedelic-induced cortical plasticity.
  2. Drewko et al., Molecular Psychiatry (2025): 5-HT₂A signalling, psychedelics and plasticity.
  3. Ketamine neuropsychiatric-mechanism reviews: NMDAR blockade, disinhibition, AMPAR, BDNF and mTORC1.
  4. Nicotine/nAChR reviews: β2-containing nAChRs, VTA dopamine circuitry and desensitization.
  5. Dolotov et al. and related Semax work: rat hippocampal/basal-forebrain BDNF and TrkB changes.
  6. Farmer et al. / nuciferine pharmacology: 5-HT and dopamine receptor actions of nuciferine; forensic blue-lotus analyses.
  7. Neural-stem-cell transplantation reviews: graft survival, immune recognition and tumorigenicity concerns.
Scope: This is a hypothesis-exploration tool, not a clinical model, toxicology calculator, pharmacokinetic model, or experimental protocol. There is no empirical dataset for simultaneous direct-brain exposure to this combination. Numerical outputs are therefore useful only for comparing internal model scenarios and identifying plausible mechanistic conflicts.
