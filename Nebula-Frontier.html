How Nebula Frontier works

This is an original ruleset inspired by classic connected-feature tile games and sci‑fi variants, not a reproduction of any licensed board game.

1 · Place a sectorEdges touching existing sectors must match: void to void, lane to lane, nebula to nebula. Green targets work at the current angle. Amber targets are legal in another orientation: click them to auto-rotate and place, or rotate manually with Q/E/R, right-click, the rotation buttons, or the exact-angle selector. Red targets cannot accept the current tile in any orientation.
2 · Deploy an operativeAfter placement, you may deploy to a lane, nebula region, or world on the tile, but only if that connected feature does not already contain an operative.
3 · Resolve skirmishIf enabled, previously separate networks carrying rival operatives can merge. Each side rolls up to 3 dice. The highest result wins; defeated operatives return to reserve and gain compensation points.
4 · ScoreClosed lanes and nebula regions score immediately and return their operatives. Worlds score when all 8 surrounding spaces are occupied. At deck end, incomplete occupied features receive reduced scoring.
