What it tests

Initiation latency is the delay from stimulus presentation to the first typed character.

Execution IKI is the interval between subsequent keystrokes, with long interruptions excluded.

Frequency advantage compares common sequences with a less-common sample, while pseudowords help separate lexical/stimulus initiation effects from within-sequence execution.

The lab deliberately does not feed pseudoword performance into your normal weak-key scheduler.
