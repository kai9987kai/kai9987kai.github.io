The model is mechanistic but not experimentally validated for BLACK 3.0 specifically. The exact paint formulation, particle-size distribution, binder chemistry and plant-specific toxicity are proprietary or unavailable.
Equations and assumptions

Transport: ∂C/∂t = −∇·(vC) + ∇·(D∇C) − kdepC.

Pit filtration: inter-conduit transfer falls sharply when aggregate diameter exceeds hydrated pit-membrane constriction scale.

Hydraulics: conductance declines via deposited solids, embolism, puncture damage and wound-associated occlusion.

Water status: water potential is coupled to VPD, remaining leaf conductance and stomatal closure.

Injury: combines injection trauma, hydraulic stress and an explicitly uncertain phytotoxicity term.

This is an educational numerical model for exploring plausible mechanisms, not a validated predictor of a real biological experiment. Do not inject paints or other non-biological materials into living plants on the basis of this simulation.
